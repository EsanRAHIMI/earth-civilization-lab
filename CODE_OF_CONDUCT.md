# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Earth Civilization Lab is a collaborative scientific software project. Participants must be able to question methods, reproduce or refute results, and submit negative findings without harassment or retaliation.

Expected behavior: respectful and constructive scientific criticism, proper attribution, transparent disclosure of conflicts and limitations, inclusive technical discussions, and attention to data rights and participant safety.

Unacceptable behavior: harassment, discriminatory or threatening remarks, publishing someone's private information without consent, manipulating results or citations, retaliation against reviewers, deliberately misleading provenance or unauthorized access to credentials.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns **privately** to the repository maintainer through their public GitHub profile contact channel rather than opening a public issue containing personal information. The maintainer may remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or refer serious incidents to GitHub's moderation mechanisms. Decisions should be based on documented behavior rather than disagreement with scientific conclusions.

This policy applies in GitHub issues, Pull Requests, discussions and project-affiliated community spaces.
