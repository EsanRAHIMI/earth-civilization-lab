# Bitcoin: observed financial and network indicators
Source: Blockchain.com Charts API, independently hosted from the World Bank source.
Observations: USD market price, network hashrate (TH/s), daily confirmed transactions and miners' revenue (USD/day).
The data pipeline stores provider raw JSON, source URLs, retrieval times, SHA256 hashes and a typed CSV. Missing or invalid observations fail validation; no synthetic interpolation.
This is a separately tracked technology/economy dataset, **not** a validated coupling into the 1000-year civilization simulation.
Hashrate cannot be converted to energy use without mining fleet efficiency, PUE and temporal assumptions. Do not use it as direct emissions data.
No investment or policy predictions are inferred. Source: https://www.blockchain.com/explorer/api/charts_api
