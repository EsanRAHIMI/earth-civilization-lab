# Scientific protocol and limits
## Aim
Compare explicitly defined governance, mitigation and innovation policies for their *conditional* behavior in a coupled exploratory stress test. Never interpret simulation fractions as estimated real-world survival probabilities.
## Existing empirical evidence
World Bank historical panel: 1000 non-null observations, 10 countries, four indicators; frozen-2015 holdout baseline evaluated on 2016–2024. See reports/EMPIRICAL_RUN_2.md.
## Current scenario model
Stocks: population, wellbeing, ecosystem, resources, knowledge, carbon intensity, warming proxy, institutional capacity. Discrete annual updates. Scenario policy differences are chosen assumptions, not fitted effects. Shocks are synthetic and seeded. 60 runs per scenario plus parameter sensitivity.
## Pre-registered limitations
The warming state is a dimensionless proxy with no physically validated energy-balance or carbon mass accounting; do not label it degrees Celsius. Ecosystem and resources are normalized proxies. The model lacks trade, migration, fertility cohorts, political economy, spatial heterogeneity and validated threshold behavior. Thresholds (population <2 billion, wellbeing <0.25, ecosystem <0.2) are illustrative value judgments, not empirically validated collapse criteria. Passing this stress test cannot establish actual 1000-year viability.
## Next scientific gate
A. Calibrate independent historical sector models, compare rolling-origin benchmarks.
B. Implement carbon/energy and resource conservation checks with documented physical units.
C. Validate sector coupling on historical out-of-sample observations and external data sources.
D. Elicit thresholds transparently; preregister scenario and decision rules.
E. Ensemble structural model uncertainty, independent replication, and peer review.
